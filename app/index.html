        Sources & honesty

        Names, descriptions and grades are PlayStation's own words, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles before this guide was written (frozen 2026-11-18). Every guide in this app is written in our own words from the official description plus general GTA knowledge — never copied from another guide.

        Where the official description does not pin down a place or a step, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing a verified location. A guide that claims a specific spot without a source earns no trust; this guide would rather say “not pinned down yet” than guess. If a trigger is vague (for example, the Rhino trophy's joke description), the guide says what is known and what is not.

        Sources: Exophase — exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/ (names, descriptions, grades) · PSNProfiles — psnprofiles.com/trophies/43531-grand-theft-auto-vi (grades and PSN progress targets, e.g. 42 Shooting Range challenges, 250 stunts, 52 areas).

        This app is fully offline: it fetches only its own files (trophies.json), saves progress in your browser (localStorage), and makes no network request. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used anywhere — the sunset look is our own.
